Our Story

Built in Ho. Growing with the Volta Region.

Vintage Cool Natural Mineral Water is produced by Askpro Hub Ltd, a Ghanaian-owned company based in Ho, Volta Region.

We started with a simple goal: to provide quality drinking water that people, families and businesses can count on. From our production site at Desiadenyo, along the Prof. Atta Mills Highway, we serve homes, shops, offices, schools, churches, institutions and events across the region.

As we grow, our commitment remains the same: to deliver quality water, serve our customers reliably and contribute to the communities we call home.

What We Stand For

  • Quality — We strive for consistency in every batch.
  • Integrity — We keep our word and act responsibly.
  • Customer Focus — We listen, respond and put our customers first.
